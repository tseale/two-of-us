# Conventions and constraints for building the painted hill in SwiftUI

> The project's conventions and constraints for new Swift.
> Written on 22 Nov 2026 by a read-only agent, against the branch at about commit `cd2b979` (the app as `origin/main` `3583e43` plus the sky lab). Line numbers are from that day and will drift. It is working context for building the painted hill (`docs/SKY_BUILD_PLAN.md`), not a maintained document.


Read-only pass: nothing changed, nothing built (there is no Swift toolchain on this machine). The branch is `origin/main` (3583e43, 2026-11-20) plus 34 commits that touch only `design/sky-lab/`. The local `main` ref is stale (57320f5), so compare against `origin/main`.

**What shapes the plan most**
- **No gating exists.** Every merge to `main` is archived and delivered to both parents' phones. There is no feature-flag system, and debug switches are compiled out of TestFlight builds.
- **No compile check before `main`.** Nothing builds on a branch or PR. New Swift is first compiled on a Mac (`make build`) or by the Xcode Cloud archive after merge.
- **The port's techniques are firsts here.** No Metal shader, `drawingGroup`, anchor preference or keyframe animator exists in the app; `Canvas` appears twice, neither in the sky.

## 1. Targets, language mode, files and resources

**Deployment targets**
- iOS 26.0 everywhere: `project.yml:5` (default), `:23` app, `:131` widgets, `:365` notification content, `:445` unit tests, `:463` UI tests.
- watchOS 26.0: `project.yml:211` watch app, `:296` complication.
- `CLAUDE.md:9` ("iOS 17+") is stale. The plan is to hold 26.0 until a 27-only API is needed (`docs/IOS-27-UPGRADE-PLAN.md:286`).

**Swift**
- `SWIFT_VERSION: "5.0"` for every target (`project.yml:15`, repeated at `:200`, `:359`, `:436`, `:454`, `:472`).
- No strict-concurrency, upcoming-feature or other Swift flags anywhere in `project.yml`, so it is Swift 5 mode with default checking.
- The compiler is Xcode 27.x's Swift 6.4 (`docs/IOS-27-UPGRADE-PLAN.md:48`). Swift 6 mode is listed as future hygiene (`docs/IOS-27-BEST-IN-CLASS.md:227`).
- Xcode 27 rule: no declaration-site initial value on `@State` that `init` also assigns (`docs/XCODE_CLOUD.md:115-121`).
- iOS 27.1 symbols (hinge, `reservedRegions`) are deliberately unused: "an undeclared symbol fails the build even behind `#available`" (`docs/IPHONE_DUO.md:280-285`). The Duo is recognised by size classes and the display midline (`TwoOfUs/Features/Shared/PaneLayout.swift:5-9`, `:45-49`).

**New files**
- The app target takes the whole `TwoOfUs/` folder (`project.yml:24-27`) and tests take all of `TwoOfUsTests/` (`:446-447`). A new `.swift` file joins on the next `xcodegen generate`.
- The `.xcodeproj` is gitignored; `make project` regenerates it (`Makefile:32-33`) and CI does the same (`ci_scripts/ci_post_clone.sh:16-17`).
- **The trap:** widgets, watch, complication and notification extension list app files one by one (`project.yml:133-180`, `:213-258`, `:298-336`, `:367-402`). Shared files include `Colors.swift`, `Typography.swift`, `AIGlow.swift`, `DayRibbon.swift` (`:162-168`) and `SleepLanguage.swift` (`:160`, `:238`, `:324`, `:397`).
- If a shared file starts using a type from a new file, those targets stop compiling until `project.yml` lists it.
- The watch compiles shared design files against its own UIKit-free tokens (`TwoOfUsWatch/WatchDesign.swift:3-10`).

**Resources**
- Images live in `TwoOfUs/Assets.xcassets/Sky/`, a plain group with no namespace. They are referenced by string: `Image("SkyGrain")` (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/SkyBackground.swift:158`), with cloud names in a table (`:880-885`).
- Loose files under `TwoOfUs/Resources/` are bundled with no `project.yml` entry and loaded by `Bundle.main.url(forResource:)` (`TwoOfUs/Alarms/AlarmTone.swift:59`).
- **Metal:** there is no `.metal` file and no `ShaderLibrary`, `colorEffect` or `layerEffect` in the app. XcodeGen should compile a `.metal` file under `TwoOfUs/` into the app's default library, but that is unproven here.

**Asset conventions**
- Art is generated by seeded scripts committed in `scripts/`: "a rerun reproduces the committed PNGs exactly", with clouds drawn at 1.25× their largest on-screen size at @3x (`scripts/make_sky_textures.py:1-11`).
- Today's sky art is 13 PNGs, 616 KB. The whole asset catalogue is 1.1 MB and the sounds 1.4 MB.
- I found no written app-size budget.

## 3. House style

**Layout**
- `TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/` holds tokens and ambient grounds; `TwoOfUs/Store/` holds pure logic (`SolarClock`, `MoonClock`, `SleepLanguage`); `TwoOfUs/Features/<Screen>/` and `Features/Shared/` hold views and Duo geometry.
- One main type per file, with `private` helper views and shapes beside it. In `TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/SkyBackground.swift`: `SkyBackground` `:37`, `private struct SkyScene` `:110`, `private struct StarField` `:368`, `enum SkyPalette` `:498`.
- Files run long: `SkyBackground.swift` is 1,026 lines and `HomeView.swift` 2,941.

**Naming and comments**
- Caseless `enum` namespaces for tokens and maths (`AppColor`, `AppFont`, `Haptics`, `SkyPalette`, `PhonePage`).
- Names come from the design's own words (the light, the spread, `Plan`, `Look`, `Ridge`).
- A `///` paragraph sits on nearly every type and member, saying why and stating its rules; sections use `// MARK:`.

**Pure core, thin view**
- Geometry, colour and time are pure values with a test file named in the comment.
- Time enters as `at date:` or `now:` plus `calendar:` with defaults (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/SkyBackground.swift:737-738`).
- The view ticks on `TimelineView(.everyMinute)` (`:93`), with a separate 24 fps timeline only for stars at night (`:378`).

**Settings and animation**
- Settings are read with `@Environment`: `\.colorScheme`, `\.colorSchemeContrast`, `\.accessibilityReduceTransparency`, `\.accessibilityReduceMotion` (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/CradleField.swift:76-79`).
- Low Power comes from `ProcessInfo` plus its notification (`:80`, `:128-132`).
- Animations are keyed to state, not wrapped at the tap, "so CloudKit- and Siri-initiated starts animate too" (`TwoOfUs/Features/Home/HomeView.swift:428-431`).

**Previews and access**
- `#Preview("name")` at the file's foot, with fixed frames and `.preferredColorScheme` (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/CradleField.swift:391-407`); 45 across 29 files. `SkyBackground.swift` has none.
- Internal by default, `private` helpers, no `public`. Shared state is an `@Observable final class` singleton (`TwoOfUs/Support/LocalPrefs.swift:36-38`).

**Not yet in the app**
- Metal shaders, `drawingGroup`, `PreferenceKey` or anchor preferences, `KeyframeAnimator`, `sensoryFeedback`.
- `Canvas` appears only at `TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/DayRibbon.swift:86` and `TwoOfUs/Features/Home/TodayHeroCard.swift:1166`. The brief's "no `Canvas` either" (`design/sky-lab/BRIEF.md:210`) is true of the sky only.

**Examples**
- `TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/Colors.swift:54-55`: `/// Semantic color tokens. Views reference these, never raw hex.` then `enum AppColor {`
- `TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/SkyBackground.swift:89-90`: `if reduceTransparency || contrast == .increased {` then `AppColor.bg`
- `TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/CradleField.swift:203-205`: `/// Everything the field paints for one pose, as plain geometry — a value of its own rather than part of the view, so it is unit-tested without a display (`CradleFieldTests`).`

## 4. Test style

- **Framework:** XCTest only, no Swift Testing. `final class XTests: XCTestCase` with `@testable import TwoOfUs`. `@MainActor` goes on suites that touch view types or SwiftData (`TwoOfUsTests/CradleFieldTests.swift:11-12`).
- **Names are sentences stating the design rule:** `testTheSkyStaysQuietBehindTheCards` (`TwoOfUsTests/SkyPaletteTests.swift:41`), `testTheDarkSkyStaysDeepBehindTheCards` (`:201`).
- **Dates:** there is no clock protocol. Tests build fixed dates with private helpers and pass them to `now:` or `at:`:
  - UTC ISO strings (`TwoOfUsTests/SkyPaletteTests.swift:335`);
  - a calendar with a named time zone (`TwoOfUsTests/SolarClockTests.swift:5-13`);
  - a fixed epoch plus `at(hours)` (`TwoOfUsTests/NightReportTests.swift:5-12`).
- **Assertions:** `XCTAssertEqual(…, accuracy:)`, `try XCTUnwrap`, and custom helpers that forward `file:`/`line:` (`TwoOfUsTests/CradleFieldTests.swift:19`). Design invariants are written as inequalities, and device sizes as literals (951 × 669, 466 × 678, the 84 pt strip).
- **SwiftData:** `AppModelContainer.make(inMemory: true)` in `setUp` (`TwoOfUsTests/DayRibbonTests.swift:17`).
- **Ported maths must match:**
  - `TwoOfUsTests/MoonClockTests.swift:9-16` checks SunCalc's own reference values to 1e-9.
  - `TwoOfUsTests/SolarClockTests.swift:22-28` checks real sunrise and sunset to ±5 min.
  - `design/sky-lab/tools/test-math.mjs:8-18` goes the other way: it ports the Swift tests to JS and reads the Swift tables by regex (`:912-914`, `:989-1020`).

## 5. Accessibility and motion rules

**The sky today**
- Flat `AppColor.bg` under Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/SkyBackground.swift:89-90`).
- Hidden from VoiceOver and never takes a touch (`:83-84`).
- Stars hold still under Reduce Motion (`:375-376`).
- It has no Low Power rule.
- Written at `SkyBackground.swift:33-36`, `docs/DESIGN.md:51` and `design/sky-lab/BRIEF.md:419-420`.

**The new sky under Reduce Motion**
- No camera move; the looked-up still cross-fades (`design/sky-lab/BRIEF.md:126`, `:271`).
- The mockup's port notes 11 and 15 give 0.6 s and a shorter moment table.

**The Duo's light, for comparison**
- Also off in Low Power Mode; under Reduce Motion, changes cut (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/CradleField.swift:92-96`, `:171-172`; `docs/IPHONE_DUO.md:164-166`).

**App-wide**
- "Every non-essential animation drops to an opacity fade or nothing" (`DESIGN.md:301`).
- No sound, ever; haptics and visuals only (`DESIGN.md:33`).
- Dynamic Type: fonts scale through `UIFontMetrics`, capped at 1.6× (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/Typography.swift:42-45`); test at XXL (`docs/DESIGN.md:135`). The "Night N" caption should use `AppFont` or a text style.
- Contrast: AA in both appearances (`DESIGN.md:111`).

**Sky-specific contrast**
- Name ≥ 7:1 and secondary line ≥ 3:1 on the sky; in dark, nothing below the top band lighter than a card (`design/sky-lab/BRIEF.md:397-403`). The second is pinned by test (`TwoOfUsTests/SkyPaletteTests.swift:201-217`).
- iOS 27 added a user transparency slider; the app must read at both ends (`docs/IOS-27-BEST-IN-CLASS.md:110-112`).

**State of the audit**
- Every box in `docs/ACCESSIBILITY_CHECKLIST.md` is unchecked. Its Reduce Motion line is `:33`.

## 7. Release mechanics for stages

**Path to TestFlight**
- Branch, pull request, merge to `main`; Xcode Cloud archives in Release (`project.yml:487-488`); both phones get the build with nothing manual (`docs/XCODE_CLOUD.md:100-106`).
- Build numbers are stamped by CI (`ci_scripts/ci_post_clone.sh:12-13`). The version is 1.1 (`project.yml:11-13`).

**Precedent**
- Today's sky went out as a run of pull requests (#283–#292, 13–16 Nov), each merge a TestFlight build, with no flag.

**Switches that exist**
- A compile-time constant: `SnooFeature.isEnabled` (`TwoOfUs/SNOO/SnooModels.swift:3-7`).
- An Info.plist build flag: `TOUPrivateCloudComputeEnabled` (`project.yml:52-58`, read at `TwoOfUs/AI/BabyIntelligence.swift:110-112`).
- Device-local settings in `LocalPrefs` (appearance, demo mode: `TwoOfUs/Support/LocalPrefs.swift:62-71`), shown in Settings → Data (`TwoOfUs/Features/Settings/DataSettingsView.swift:11-39`).
- Debug launch arguments (`-seedSampleData`, `-uiScreen`, `-forceResumeCurtain`: `TwoOfUs/App/TwoOfUsApp.swift:19-62`, `TwoOfUs/App/RootView.swift:52-56`). These are `#if DEBUG`, so they never reach TestFlight, and several block sync (`TwoOfUs/Sync/SyncGate.swift:22`).

**What does not exist**
- No `AppConfig` type (`AppConfigTests` checks Info.plist keys), no remote config, no hidden debug menu.

**How unfinished work is kept from the parents**
- It stays on its branch, or sits in the tree unused (`docs/IPHONE_DUO.md:129-132`). A staged sky needs a switch built for it; a `LocalPrefs` toggle or a compile-time constant are the two existing shapes.

**Trying it without real data**
- Demo mode runs the app on an in-memory sample store with sync off (`TwoOfUs/Support/LocalPrefs.swift:62-66`).

## 8. Surprising or fragile

1. **"Night sleep" has three meanings.**
   - Wording says night is 8 PM–6 AM by start (`TwoOfUs/Store/SleepLanguage.swift:16-17`). The Duo's light uses that rule (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/CradleField.swift:180-184`).
   - The night schedule has its own window, and the design's is 6 pm–4 am (`design/sky-lab/BRIEF.md:220-224`).
   - `SleepLanguage.swift` compiles into five targets, so a new rule is safer in a new app-only file.
2. **First light at −7° has no maths yet.** `SolarClock` gives sunrise, sunset and progress only (`TwoOfUs/Store/SolarClock.swift:19`, `:47`, `:61`). Sun coordinates exist privately inside `MoonClock` (`TwoOfUs/Store/MoonClock.swift:198`).
3. **"By hand" is only partly recorded.**
   - `sourceRaw == nil` means logged by hand (`TwoOfUs/Models/SleepEvent.swift:18-21`), but not on which phone, nor whether by Siri or a widget.
   - Home's own tap is `startSleep()` (`TwoOfUs/Features/Home/HomeView.swift:2488-2493`).
4. **The tap collides with existing behaviour.** That tap also raises a 3-second Undo toast, and Undo cancels the sleep mid-moment. The Sleep tile's morph into the sleep card (spring 0.45) fires at the same instant.
5. **Two skies on the spread.** The phone page's and the leading page's are separate views with separate minute clocks (`TwoOfUs/Features/Home/HomeView.swift:461-464`, `:610-611`). A camera move needs one driver.
6. **The view takes no injected time.** The sky uses `max(ctx.date, .now)` (`TwoOfUs/DesignSystem/SkyBackground.swift:94`), so no other hour can be shown on a device or in a preview without adding a debug hook.
7. **Rewriting `SkyBackground.swift` breaks two checks:** the 27 palette tests and the lab's regex comparison (see §6). Decide whether the old sky stays as a baseline or both are retired.
8. **The cover and the spread differ by 9 pt in height** (678 vs 669), and everything is laid out from the top (`docs/IPHONE_DUO.md:74`).
9. **A compile error on `main` stops the next TestFlight build for every change.** Code inside `#if DEBUG` is not compiled by the archive.

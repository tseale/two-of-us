Banner: the whiteboard, above the fold

  • Title, a one-line hint that holding it reveals the button, then the rows: last bottle, last diaper, wake time with the overnight total, your notes since last night.
  • iOS shows four body lines on the lock screen, so empty rows are left out rather than dashed, wake and overnight share a line, and a long note is cut to about one line with an ellipsis. The full note is on the card.
  • With other notifications stacked, iOS collapses every app's body to two lines; tapping the stack brings the full banner back. That's a system behaviour, not ours.

Card: the rows big, one button

  • Holding the banner shows the same five rows at reading size, re-read live from the shared store, so a bottle logged in the car after the prompt fired still shows.
  • Dropped off is the only button. It logs the check-in in the background, syncs it to the other phone right away, and dismisses the notification. The app never opens. Tapping the card itself opens the app as before.
  • The pick-up prompt is unchanged: a plain "Picked up" button.

What's new vs. today

  • The body used to say "You're at Daycare — log the drop-off with one tap." It is now the whiteboard: last bottle, last diaper, wake time with the overnight total, and your own notes since last night.
  • Rows with nothing logged are left out instead of shown as a dash, so the four core answers fit the lock screen's four body lines. Wake time and overnight sleep share a line for the same reason.
  • Long-pressing now opens a rich card (the notification content extension handles this category too) with the five rows at reading size, re-read live from the shared store.

The Dropped off button

Already existed; unchanged. It runs in the background: it writes the check-in through QuickLogger, syncs it to the other phone, and the notification dismisses. The app never opens. Tapping the notification body itself still opens the app as before, and the in-app slide-to-drop-off still shows the full-screen whiteboard card.

Where the numbers come from

One builder (DropoffBoard) feeds both the in-app card and this notification, using the same rules: newest live bottle and diaper, the newest ended sleep's end as wake time, sleep overlap with the configured night window (default 8 PM to 8 AM) as the overnight total, and parents' notes since that window opened, oldest first. Daycare-sourced notes are excluded.
